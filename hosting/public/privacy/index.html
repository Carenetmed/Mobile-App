Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-27 · Effective 27 September 2026

Information we process

CareNet processes account and profile information, professional verification information, appointment and availability records, consultation communications, payment records, device registration information, and security and operational logs needed to provide and protect the service.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate accounts, operate consultations, enforce account authority, process authorized transactions, deliver transactional updates, prevent abuse, support users, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

Information is shared only as needed with the patient or professional involved in a consultation and with service providers that operate CareNet infrastructure. We do not publish private verification documents or credentials.

Retention and deletion

Account deletion deactivates access and revokes sessions. Some records may be retained where required for security, financial, dispute, audit, or legal obligations. See account deletion information.

Your choices

You can manage profile information, notification preferences, security, and account deletion from the app where available. Use the Support page for help.

Security

CareNet uses access controls, encrypted transport, and operational safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security.
