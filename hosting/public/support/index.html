CareNet

Support

For account, appointment, payment, messaging, or consultation problems, first retry the affected action and preserve the time and appointment identifier shown in CareNet.

Account access

Use the email verification and password reset links sent by CareNet. Never share passwords, verification links, reset links, or payment credentials.

Appointments and consultations

Use the appointment screen to check the authoritative status. A request is not confirmed until accepted. Do not send health information through channels outside the consultation workflow.

Urgent safety

CareNet is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services or go to the nearest appropriate emergency facility for urgent help.

Contact availability

Direct support contact and service hours are being finalized for launch. Until then, use only support options published inside the signed CareNet application. CareNet will never ask for your password or one-time verification link.
