Account deletion

Signed-in users can request deletion from Account → Delete account in the CareNet app.

Before deletion

CareNet requires recent authentication and an explicit confirmation. This protects accounts from unauthorized deletion.

What happens

The account is deactivated and active sessions are revoked. Access to authenticated CareNet features ends immediately after the request succeeds.

Records that may remain

Some appointment, consultation, transaction, security, dispute, and audit records may be retained when required for legal, financial, safety, or integrity purposes. Retained records remain access-controlled and are not used to restore normal account access.

Need help?

If you cannot access the in-app deletion control, review the current instructions on the Support page. Do not send passwords or verification links.
